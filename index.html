Professional summary

I'm a Design Manager who leads creative teams of up to 10 people. I run structured processes with measurable KPIs, clear resource allocation and efficient delivery in corporate, deadline-driven environments.

I stay hands-on. When timelines are tight or capacity runs short, I step into the work myself, clear the bottleneck and keep the team's performance sustainable.

I treat visual storytelling as a business tool: turning briefs into well-structured visuals that follow brand strategy across digital, graphic and integrated touchpoints.

I also design UI for apps and websites in Figma, building layouts, components and screens that carry the brand into digital products.

AI is part of my daily design workflow, from image generation and video editing to automating graphics-related tasks. I regularly use Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini alongside one another, choosing the right tool for each task. I’m comfortable learning new AI tools and would welcome the opportunity to explore them further with the company’s support.
